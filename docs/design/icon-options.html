Icon options

Four marks drawn from the Critical Edition world, each at Store, desktop and tray sizes.

aThe bracket

The apparatus mark, “]”, where one reading meets another: what the app shows on every review. Reads at tray size as a single white stroke.

bThe margin

A page with a highlighted line and its line number in rubric red: checking a text line by line. Richest at large sizes; the number disappears below 24 px.

cTwo witnesses

The source behind, the reading text in front, one word marked where they disagree: the side-by-side review in one picture.

dThe initial

The name's first letter in Garamond with a rubric point: quiet and typographic, and it changes with the name if the name changes.

Names, if Scriptorium should change

Collate
To compare copies of a text against each other and the original: the app's daily work. A verb, easy to say in English, French and German.
Witness
An edition's word for each copy of a text. Here every model's reading is a witness checked against the source.
Recto
The front of a leaf. Short, archival, unused in this category; says “documents” more than “recordings”.
Lemma
The word in the text that a variant comments on. Precise and memorable, but obscure outside textual scholarship.
Scriptorium
Kept: the copying room where texts were made and checked. Familiar to historians; like any of these names, it needs a trademark check before the Store listing.
