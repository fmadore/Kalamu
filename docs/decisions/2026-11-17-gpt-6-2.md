# 2026-11-17 · GPT-6.2 Sol and Astra in place of 6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra

## Context

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Sol (`gpt-6.2-sol`) and GPT-6.2 Astra (`gpt-6.2-astra`) on 17 November
2026 ([announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/)). Its model list
now names them its two flagship models, beside GPT-6.1 Terra and GPT-6 Luna; GPT-6.1 Sol and
GPT-6 Astra are still served and appear on no deprecation list.

- **GPT-6.2 Sol** keeps 6.1 Sol's prices: $2 / $10 per million tokens, $0.10 cached, $2.50 to
  write the cache, half in batch mode.
- **GPT-6.2 Astra** costs a fifth less than GPT-6 Astra: $8 / $40 (from $10 / $50), $0.40
  cached (from $1), $10 to write the cache (from $12.50), half in batch mode.
- Both read text and images, write up to 128,000 tokens, and take the levels `low`, `medium`,
  `high`, `xhigh` and `max`; `none` is refused, as it was by the models they follow. OpenAI
  advises `medium` to start with, and `low` in place of `none` or `minimal`.
- Above 272,000 input tokens a request costs twice its input and one and a half times its
  output, as for GPT-6.1; Kalamu sends one piece per request, far below it.
- From GPT-6.2 the API drops reasoning items when a conversation's history changes. Kalamu
  sends single requests and keeps no conversation, so nothing changes for it.

## Decision

- **Both are catalogue entries, and nothing else changed**: no code names a model. Each takes
  its predecessor's inputs, limits, levels and tier.
- **`successor` on GPT-6.1 Sol and on GPT-6 Astra**, by the maintainer's choice rather than
  OpenAI's word, as for Gemini on [16 November](2026-11-16-older-gemini-lines.md): the newer
  model costs the same or less. New job no longer offers the two older ones; a pipeline,
  workflow step or comparison that names one keeps it, marked *replaced*, with its replacement
  a click away.
- **GPT-6.2 Sol is OpenAI's default** for text extraction and analysis, in 6.1 Sol's place.
- GPT-6.1 Terra and GPT-6 Luna are unchanged.

## Consequences

- A new job on OpenAI's default costs what it did; one on Astra costs a fifth less.
- Neither model has read a real page in Kalamu yet: their descriptions say so for Sol, and the
  roadmap's live runs list both, handwriting and refusals included. OpenAI launches them with
  tighter safeguards than their predecessors, which is one more reason to try them on
  historical material before relying on them.
- An installed copy learns of both from the model list it downloads, without an update.
