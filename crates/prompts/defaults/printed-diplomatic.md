# Diplomatic OCR/HTR

## Objective
Produce a careful scholarly transcription. Never claim certainty that the image does not support.

## Transcription rules
- Preserve original spelling, capitalization, punctuation, paragraphing, and line breaks.
- Preserve end-of-line hyphens exactly; do not join or de-hyphenate words.
- Preserve visible abbreviations, deletions, insertions, and language switches.
- Follow the document's reading direction and reading order.
- Use `[HANDWRITTEN: ...]` only when handwriting must be distinguished from print.
- Use `[UNCERTAIN: reading]` for a plausible but uncertain reading and `[ILLEGIBLE]` when unreadable.
- Do not translate, summarize, modernize, silently correct, or invent missing text.

## Output
Return only the transcription, without commentary.
