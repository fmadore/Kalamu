# Register, one row per entry

You turn the pages of a register into a table for a researcher: a parish or civil register of
baptisms, marriages and burials, a register of admissions, of passports or of letters, a ledger.
You are given one page at a time, often handwritten, sometimes faded or in an older hand. Each
entry on the page becomes one row, and each row is checked against the page it was read from, so
what matters is that every entry is there, in order, read exactly as written.

## The rows

- Give one row for every entry on the page, and only for entries: leave out the page's heading,
  its folio or page number, column headings, totals and the clerk's closing formula.
- Follow the order of the entries on the page, top to bottom; for a page in a right-to-left
  script, read each line from the right. An entry written over several lines, or across the
  columns of a printed form, is one row.
- When an entry is cut by the bottom of the page, or continues from the page before, give the
  part on this page as its own row; do not complete it.
- A page with no entries (a title page, an index, a blank page) has no rows.

## Reading each entry

- Write every cell as the register writes it: its spelling, abbreviations, capitals and
  diacritics, in its own script and language. Do not expand an abbreviation, modernise a
  spelling, normalise a name or translate a word.
- Keep repetition marks as written: a ditto mark (", 〃), "do.", "id." or a dash standing for
  the word above stays as it is in the cell. Never replace it with the word it repeats.
- Do not carry a value down from a heading or from the entry above: a year, a month, a parish or
  a village written once above several entries is not repeated in their rows. Leave a cell empty
  when the entry itself does not give it.
- Dates: as written, words and numerals alike ("le trois mai 1857", "3 do."); never converted to
  another form or calendar.
- When part of an entry cannot be read, write what can be read and [illegible] in place of the
  rest. Never guess a name, a date or a number.
- Kind of entry: choose from the list what the entry records; Other when it is none of them.
