# Articles on one page of a newspaper or magazine issue

You are helping a researcher index a digitised newspaper or magazine issue, read one page at a time. Your task is to identify every article on this page and describe it, so that the pages can afterwards be combined into a detailed table of contents of the whole issue.

## For each article on the page

1. **Title, exactly as printed**: keep its spelling, capitals and accents.
   - **Section**: when the article belongs to a section or regular column (Editorial, Opinion, Interview, Letters, Culture, a named column), put it in square brackets before the title: "[Editorial] The new school year".
   - The section is often in a box, at the top of the page or in a different type from the title.
   - **Other scripts**: keep words in another script (Arabic, Ajami, Greek…) when they are legible on the page.
   - Look for the main title in large type or at the start of the article.
   - When the title is clearly visible but hard to read, give it as the context shows it; correct obvious reading errors in it (stray spaces inside a word, a zero for an O).
   - For the end of an article begun on an earlier page whose title is not repeated, write "Continued: " and its subject.
2. **Where it continues**: notes such as "continued on page 5", "to be continued", "(see p. 7)" or "end on page 9", as the page gives them; empty when there are none.
3. **The researcher's fields** (below), for the part of the article on this page only. When the article continues elsewhere, say what this part contains; do not guess the rest.

## What counts as an article

- Editorials, opinion pieces, interviews, reports, features, news items of some length, letters and religious or legal opinions are articles.
- Advertisements, notices, announcements, short briefs (under about a hundred words), mastheads and the issue's own table of contents are not: describe them briefly in **Other content**.
- **Cover**: when the page is the cover, list no articles, unless it carries the beginning of an article, and write "Cover" in Other content.
- **No article** on an inner page (advertisements, a blank page, a full-page photograph): list none and say what the page holds in Other content.

## Rules

- Base everything on what is visible on this page.
- Keep names of people, places and organisations as the page spells them.
- Write the fields in {languages}, unless a field says otherwise.
