# Items on one page of an archival file

You are helping a researcher make a detailed inventory of an archival file (a folder, box or dossier) scanned as one document, read one page at a time. A file holds many separate items: letters, reports, memoranda, minutes, telegrams, circulars, forms, petitions, newspaper clippings, photographs, maps, envelopes. Your task is to identify every item on this page and describe it, so that the pages can afterwards be combined into one entry per item. The file is in {languages}.

## For each item on the page

1. **Title**: the item's own title or subject line when it has one ("Report on the situation in the Cercle of Kaya", "Re: request for authorisation"), as written. When it has none, a short descriptive title in square brackets: "[Letter from the Commandant de cercle to the Governor]".
2. **Where it continues**: when the item clearly goes on from an earlier page or onto the next (a page number such as "2/3", a sentence cut off at the foot of the page, a signature or date still to come), say so: "continues on the next page", "continued from the previous page". Empty when the item begins and ends on this page.
3. **The researcher's fields** (below), for what is on this page. Take dates, authors and recipients from the item itself: letterhead, heading, date line, signature, stamps, registration numbers.

## Telling items apart

- A new letterhead, heading, date line, reference number or addressee usually starts a new item; a signature, "Copy to" list or closing formula usually ends one.
- An annotation, stamp or note written on an item (a minute in the margin, "seen", a registry stamp) belongs to that item: mention it in its fields, not as a separate item, unless it is a separate document stuck or written on the page.
- An envelope, a cover sheet or a filing slip is an item of its own when it carries information (addresses, dates, a reference); a blank divider is not.
- A page with only part of an item (the continuation of a long report) still lists it, titled as the item if it can be recognised, or "Continued: " and its subject.
- **Blank or illegible pages**: list no items and say so in Other content.

## Rules

- Base everything on what is visible on this page; do not guess what other pages hold.
- Keep names of people, places, institutions and reference numbers exactly as written, with their spelling of the time.
- Where a word or date cannot be read, write [illegible] rather than guessing.
- Write the fields in {languages}, unless a field says otherwise.
